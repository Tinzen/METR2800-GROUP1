First tune, step by step

  1. Open robot folder (top) and pick METR2800-GROUP1. Until then you're on a read-only copy. Save (Ctrl+S) writes Sequence.h.
  2. Watch the run: pick Whole run and press ▶ Play (or Space). The strip under the step names shows the scoop: green holds rocks, yellow level, red pours. Issues (top) lists anything that looks wrong: click one to go to it.
  3. Pick a step: click its block on the timeline, use ‹ › in the Step tab, or ← →. The Step tab says what it does in plain words. Click the timeline and hold → to play from there at the play speed (← = backwards).
  4. Tune it: drag its ◆ on the timeline (up/down = position, sideways = when it arrives), or click a value box in the Step tab and press ↑ ↓ (Shift ×10, Alt ×0.2). Up/down keeps the move's timing: its speed (and the next move's on that servo) is re-solved, so nothing else on the timeline moves. Drag a step block's right edge to change how long it takes. Ctrl+Z undoes.
  5. Check it: pick This step and press ▶ Play. ⏮ goes back to where the step starts, ⏭ to where its moves finish.
  6. Try it on the robot, no upload: Connect robot, then Drives: Robot · live, then ▶ Play. The robot lines up, then copies the twin. Every run is saved to logs/ and drawn dashed on the timeline, so you can compare it with the prediction.
  7. Save and upload Robot_Controller_V2_1.ino. Drives: Robot · uploaded plays the copy in the robot's memory, exactly as at the competition.

Good to know

Shared names. A name (A_PEAK, S_DOWN…) can be used by several steps, and changing it changes all of them. The Step tab and the ◆ show ×2 when that's the case; Change this step only gives the step its own copy.

HOLD = that axis keeps doing what it was doing, so a move started in an earlier step keeps going. Every move is a smooth profile; a new target takes over a running move smoothly.

Next step: after ms · all arrived ± ms (every axis has stopped: arm, scoop and slides, including moves started in earlier steps) · arm / scoop / slides arrived ± ms (that axis only) · arm / scoop / slides at % of its move. + ms waits that long after arriving; − ms goes that long before it arrives (by the plan), so the next move starts early and blends in. Two slide steps in a row going the same way blend without stopping.

Or after (ms, 0 = off): the step moves on after this long even if its condition never comes true (a stuck slide, a stalled move). The robot logs E,STEP_LIMIT; the timeline marks the step ⏱.

Speeds: servos in °/s (default ARM_SPEED / SCOOP_SPEED; ARM_FAST / SCOOP_FAST = the servo's own top speed). Slides pick a named speed; SLIDE_RETRACT_SPEED uses SLIDE_RETRACT_ACCEL / _DECEL. Every axis has an ACCEL (speeding up) and a DECEL (slowing down) in Angles & values. For one servo move, click a/d next to its speed in the Step tab (or the acc / dec boxes in the table): a % of the axis's values (100 = those).

Slides by hand (Angles & values, connected): hold ◀ In / Out ▶ to drive them gently, Zero here makes where they are 0 mm, and letting go of the SLIDES slider sends them there.

Drives: Twin only moves nothing real. Robot · live streams the twin's pose 25 times a second (timing comes from the editor's model). Robot · uploaded runs the robot's own copy; Pause each step stops it after every step and Continue runs the next.

Safety and the robot link

■ Stop (top right, or X) stops playback, the robot's run, the slides (they go loose) and the shaking. Limp lets everything go so you can move it by hand. ↺ Reset sends the robot to the IDLE pose with the slides in.

When you press Connect robot the robot holds still and waits (▾ next to it changes that). You never need to change RUN_MODE in the code. With no computer attached it runs as normal.

3D view: locked by default so a stray drag doesn't spin it. Unlocked: scroll zooms at the cursor, middle-drag pans, Shift + middle-drag orbits, double-click fits.

Without the folder (other browsers): edit, then ⋯ → Download or Copy, and replace Sequence.h. Tell your agent if you did, so the files stay in sync. Full walkthrough: SEQUENCE_TUNING.md. The twin's angles are only as good as the servo calibration (Calibrate servos).

The whole state machine as a table: each axis's target with that move's speed under it. Hover a cell to change it; drag ⠿ to reorder.
