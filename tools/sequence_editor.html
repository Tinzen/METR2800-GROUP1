Run on the robot

The robot runs the sequence it was uploaded with. Save and re-upload after editing.

Serial

  1. Open robot folder and pick the repo (or Robot_Controller_V2_1). Save writes straight back to Sequence.h and RobotConfig.h.
  2. Watch it: the play button on the twin (or Space). ‹ › (or the arrow keys) jump one step. The strip under the timeline shows whether the scoop holds rocks (green), is level (yellow) or pours (red).
  3. Change what a step does: click its number or description. The Step tab says what it does in plain words and lets you change it with menus. Drag the edge between two blocks on the timeline to change how long a step takes; drag ⠿ in the table to reorder.
  4. Change a pose: Angles & values → Show on a name, then Jog the sliders until the twin looks right → Capture. No robot needed. While you edit or jog, the grey ghost shows the value as it is now.
  5. Save (Ctrl+S), upload Robot_Controller_V2_1.ino, then check on the real robot: the same sliders move it when the live link is connected.

Full walkthrough: SEQUENCE_TUNING.md in the repo. The twin's angles are only as good as the servo calibration (Calibration tab).

3D view (Fusion 360 / Inventor): scroll zooms at the cursor, middle-drag pans, Shift + middle-drag orbits about the point under the cursor, double-click fits the robot.

Step rules: ARM / SCOOP take an angle name, a number or HOLD. HOLD keeps whatever that axis is doing, so a move started earlier keeps going. MOVE is INSTANT (V2 jump), PROFILED (smooth) or a time in ms (arm and scoop finish together; ARM VIA makes the arm go through that angle halfway). NEXT WHEN decides when the next step starts. Two slide steps in a row in the same direction blend without stopping.

Without the folder (other browsers): edit, then Download or Copy and replace Sequence.h. Tell your agent if you did, so the files stay in sync.
